This Privacy Policy describes how YourGame ("we", "our", or "us") handles information when you use our mobile game ("the App"). We are committed to being transparent about our data practices.

Information We Collect

We do not directly collect personal information from users. However, the third-party advertising and analytics SDKs integrated into our App may collect the following:

  • Device identifiers (e.g. Advertising ID, IDFA)
  • IP address and approximate location
  • Device type, OS version, and language
  • In-app interactions and session data for analytics
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)

How We Use Information

Any data collected by third-party SDKs is used to:

  • Display relevant advertisements
  • Measure ad performance and campaign effectiveness
  • Improve app stability and user experience
  • Comply with legal obligations

Third-Party Services

Our App uses Yodo1 MAS (Managed Ads Service) for ad monetization, which integrates multiple ad networks including Google AdMob, AppLovin, Meta Audience Network, Unity Ads, IronSource, and others. Each of these services operates under its own privacy policy. We encourage you to review them:

  • Google / AdMob Privacy Policy
  • AppLovin Privacy Policy
  • Meta Privacy Policy
  • Unity Privacy Policy
  • IronSource Privacy Policy
  • Yodo1 Privacy Policy

Children's Privacy

Our App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through our App, please contact us and we will take steps to delete that information.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access or delete personal data collected about you
  • Opt out of targeted advertising (via your device's ad settings)
  • Withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent

On Android, you can reset or opt out of personalized ads via Settings → Google → Ads. On iOS, go to Settings → Privacy → Tracking.

Data Retention

We do not store personal data on our own servers. Third-party SDKs retain data according to their own policies. Please refer to the respective privacy policies linked above.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information collected through our App. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
